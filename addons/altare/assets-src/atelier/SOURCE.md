Each strip fills its own rectangular cell entirely with its own textile; no transparent region, NO scalloped cutout yet (the game compiler will generate the cut), no shadows at cell edges. Keep all emblems between 15 and 75 percent of cell height and within the middle 30 percent of the right cell width. Same fine thread density and even diffuse illumination throughout. Straight-on orthographic flat textile scan, high craftsmanship, precise raised stitches, gentle thread-scale relief, no perspective, no draping, no altar, no room, no vessels, no watermark, no branding, no text except IHS.
